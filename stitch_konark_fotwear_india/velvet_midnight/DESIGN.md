## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a nocturnal, tactile luxury aesthetic that merges high-fashion editorial craftsmanship with modern high-performance design. Inspired by deep midnight skies, heavy woven velvet textures, and luminous chromatic highlights, the visual language balances aristocratic richness with technical precision. 

The aesthetic is Tactile & Editorial Dark Minimalism. Surfaces evoke physical textile density through subtle luminosity, dual-cast shadows, and fine metallic sheen. The target audience seeks exclusivity, understated power, and bespoke craft—demanding a sensory, immersive experience that feels as curated as a high-end luxury atelier or couture flagship.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an opulent midnight dark spectrum, pairing intense jewel tones with ambient nocturnal depths:

- **Primary (`#2A52BE` - Royal Sapphire):** A vibrant, saturated royal blue commanding focal points, primary action triggers, and brand emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#134E4A` - Deep Velvet Pine / Emerald):** A rich, shadowed jade-teal providing lush velvet layering, complementary container fills, and rich low-lit atmosphere.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Pale Gilded Amber):** A luminous warm metallic accent used sparingly for badges, prestige markers, fine borders, and key interactive highlights.
- **Neutral (`#080B10` - Obsidian Midnight):** A warm, deep blue-black base canvas that preserves deep black contrast while absorbing rich chromatic reflections.
- **Functional Accents:** Mint Frost (`#6EE7B7`) for active success states and crisp telemetry tags; Deep Garnet (`#E11D48`) reserved for destructive actions.

## Typography

The type system creates tension between classic high-fashion editorial presence and modern technical poise:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** High-contrast transitional serif delivering dramatic grandeur, sharp serifs, and architectural rhythm across marquee headers and editorial hero moments.
- **Body & Titles (`Manrope`):** Modern geometric-humanist sans offering crystal clarity, balanced aperture, and effortless readability against deep velvet backgrounds.
- **Metadata & Labels (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical geometric mono-proportional voice with wide tracking for tags, micro-labels, pricing tiers, and interface indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a structured 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column sequence with a 4-column sub-grid on mobile screens:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max container width of 1320px with 48px (`margin`) outer margins and 24px (`gutter`) column spacing, allowing generous negative space to enhance the luxury feel.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 32px margins and 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px (`margin-mobile`) margins and 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Horizontal padding compresses gracefully while vertical breathing space between sections remains prominent (`space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids stark drop shadows in favor of ambient chromatic glow, velvet light falloff, and subtle physical boundaries:

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Pure `#080B10` with an imperceptible radial gradient bloom of `#0F1D38` toward screen centers or hero assets.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Dark jade-slate container (`#0C161D`) reinforced by a micro-border of `1px solid rgba(42, 82, 190, 0.18)` and an ultra-soft dual shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(19, 78, 74, 0.25)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Floating Overlays):** Deep navy glass surface (`rgba(10, 18, 30, 0.85)`) with `16px` backdrop blur, edged with a fine rim light: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Key Popovers):** Elevated surface with an intense diffuse halo of sapphire and emerald: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(42, 82, 190, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a balanced `Rounded` (Level 2) shape language:

- Standard interactive controls, inputs, and list modules carry an 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius, projecting architectural structure without harshness.
- Cards, modal viewports, and showcase media utilize 16px (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`) radii.
- Hero containers and focal callout modules step up to 24px (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`).
- Chips, operational status badges, and circular action pills use full encapsulation (`9999px`) to visually soften technical data.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Royal Sapphire background (`#2A52BE`) with high-density text in `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner bevel highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`), and a soft sapphire ambient bloom on hover (`0 0 20px rgba(42, 82, 190, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Velvet Ghost:** Deep jade surface (`rgba(19, 78, 74, 0.35)`) framed by `1px solid rgba(42, 82, 190, 0.4)`, transitioning to pale gold border accents on focus.
- **Tertiary Accent:** Minimal dark pill with Gilded Gold text (`#D4AF37`) and subtle underline indicator.

### Cards & Modules
- Structured containers with midnight jade-black fill (`#0A1218`) and multi-layer borders (a 1px hairline perimeter of sapphire fading into deep teal).
- Card headers pair `Bodoni Moda` titles with uppercase `Space Grotesk` technical category kickers.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Dark inset frames (`#05080C`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Active focus state ignites a soft Royal Sapphire ring with an internal teal gradient glow, accompanied by amber text cursors.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill geometries styled with velvet emerald translucent tinting (`rgba(19, 78, 74, 0.4)`) bordered by mint frost (`rgba(110, 231, 183, 0.3)`), set with tracked monospace labels.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp 8px-rounded squares and circles with a 1.5px sapphire outline. Checked states display solid royal blue fills with gilded amber or white micro-glyphs.

### Supplementary Components (Hero Showcase & Product Carousels)
- Designed with deep contrast framing, ambient backlight reflections simulating theatrical stage lighting, and gilded micro-pagination dots.
