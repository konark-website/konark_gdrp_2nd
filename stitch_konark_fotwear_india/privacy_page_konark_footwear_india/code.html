Clause 01 Binding B2B Framework

Preamble & Scope of Commercial Agreement

This document establishes the binding general commercial conditions, manufacturing requirements, and export regulations between Konark Footwear India (“Manufacturer”, “Company”, “We”) and any buying entity, OEM footwear brand, wholesale distributor, buying house, or multinational footwear enterprise (“Buyer”, “Commercial Client”).

Issuance of a formal Purchase Order (PO), pro-forma invoice endorsement, deposit transmission, or delivery of CAD tooling files implies full and irrevocable acceptance of these Terms. Standard commercial runs operate strictly under an industrial minimum order quantity threshold of 5,000 pairs per specification, density tier, and color scheme.

Non-Disclosure of Tooling & Chemical Formulations

CAD schematics, mold blue-prints, proprietary laser perforations, and client-specific high-rebound polyurethane (PU) or ethylene-vinyl acetate (EVA) density recipes (ranging Shore A 30 to Shore A 60) submitted to Konark Footwear India are protected under rigorous bilateral confidentiality covenants. Production samples are never re-engineered, exhibited, or supplied to third parties without prior express written sanction.

Clause 03 Production Specifications

Order Placement, Minimum Order Quantities & Sampling

1. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): To uphold automated conveyor calibration, CNC aluminum mold mounting, and high-pressure chemical mix integrity, the minimum operational run is 5,000 pairs per colorway and sole profile. Aggregate multi-size splits must conform to client master cartons of 50 to 100 pairs.

2. Pre-Production Prototype Approval: Before triggering industrial casting, an initial counter-sample (Golden Sample) will be fabricated and dispatched via express air courier to Buyer’s headquarters. Full production commences only upon formal written acceptance or signed return of the counter-sample.

3. Manufacturing Tolerances: Insoles and orthopedic orthotics are subject to standard international elastomeric manufacturing tolerances: thickness variance ±0.3mm, hardness ±2 Shore C/A, and color variance within ΔE ≤ 1.2 on the CIELAB scale against agreed master pantone swatches.

Clause 04 Financial & Customs Instruments

Payment Terms, Banking & Export Documentation

Standard export operations adhere strictly to the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600) and accepted international commercial banking protocols:

  • Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC): 100% at sight issued through a first-class international bank, confirmed by our nominated correspondent bank in New Delhi, India.
  • Telegraphic Transfer (TT / SWIFT): 30% advance deposit upon Purchase Order confirmation for raw material compounding and tooling setup; 70% balance payable upon submission of copy Bill of Lading (BL) and pre-shipment SATRA inspection certificate.
  • Customs & DGFT Documentation: Konark Footwear India delivers full commercial packages comprising Commercial Invoice, Detailed Packing List with net/gross weights, Certificate of Origin (COO issued under Indo-EU trade guidelines where applicable), Fumigation Certificates for palletized freight, and Marine Cargo insurance policies.
Clause 05 Testing & Pre-Shipment Audit

Quality Assurance, SATRA Testing & Third-Party Inspection

Every export consignment undergoes stringent in-house QA protocols aligned with SATRA Technology Center test procedures for footwear components:

Tensile & Rebound ASTM D3574 test metrics verifying rebound resilience greater than 55%.
Compression Set Under 8% permanent deformation after 50,000 standard flex-cycles.
Abrasion & Fastness SATRA TM31 dry & wet rubbing resistance for premium top cloths.

Third-Party Inspection Rights: Commercial buyers maintain the unreserved right to commission certified inspection agencies (including SGS, Intertek, TÜV SÜD, or Bureau Veritas) to perform ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 (ISO 2859-1) AQL 1.5/4.0 pre-shipment container audits at our Mundka, Delhi facility prior to sealing and container departure.

Clause 06 Incoterms® 2020 Rules

Shipping, Freight Routing & Risk Transfer

Unless otherwise negotiated in writing via formal contract, goods are quoted under Incoterms® 2020:

  • FOB (Free on Board): Inland Container Depot (ICD TKD Delhi / ICD Dadri) or Sea Ports: JNPT / Nhava Sheva (Mumbai) and Mundra Port (Gujarat). Risk of loss transfers as cargo passes the ship’s rail or enters carrier terminal possession.
  • CIF / CFR (Cost, Insurance and Freight): Direct ocean shipping to major destination hubs including Hamburg, Rotterdam, Valencia, Felixstowe, Dubai, and Long Beach. Marine cargo insurance covers Institute Cargo Clauses (A) all-risks baseline.
  • Lead Times: Standard production cycle ranges from 21 to 35 business days from final sample sign-off and receipt of operative LC/deposit. Container ocean transit schedules are carrier-dependent and outside direct manufacturer control.

Clause 08 Commercial Warranty

Warranties, Liability Limitation & Force Majeure

Manufacturing Warranty: Konark Footwear India provides an express warranty guaranteeing that all delivered goods conform to agreed physical sample specs and remain free from delamination, foam crumbling, or chemical off-ratio defects for a duration of 90 calendar days following arrival at destination port. Defective lots exceeding 2% of the consignment will be remanufactured or credited on subsequent POs.

Cap on Liability: Under no circumstances shall either party be liable to the other for indirect, special, punitive, or consequential damages (including loss of market, brand goodwill, or retail sales cancellation). Total financial liability for any claim shall not exceed the net commercial invoice value of the affected batch.

Force Majeure: Neither entity shall be liable for defaults or delivery lags arising from circumstances beyond reasonable control, including acts of God, maritime container blockades, port strikes, embargoes, export control revisions, pandemics, or raw chemical shortages induced by global petrochemical plant shutdowns.

Clause 09 Legal Jurisdiction

Governing Law, Arbitration & Venue

These Terms and any individual supply agreements executed hereunder shall be interpreted and governed in accordance with the substantive laws of India, excluding its conflicts of laws provisions.

All disputes, controversies, or claims arising out of or relating to commercial shipments shall initially be submitted to good-faith executive mediation. If unresolved within 30 days, matters shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) or Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be New Delhi, India, and proceedings conducted in the English language. Competent courts in New Delhi retain exclusive jurisdiction for provisional relief.
