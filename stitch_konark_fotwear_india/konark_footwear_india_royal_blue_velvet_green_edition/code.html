About Konark Footwear India

Industrial Insole Engineering & Global OEM Export • Mundka, New Delhi

1870 Generational Heritage
50+ Export Destinations
SATRA Tested & Compliant
5,000+ Min Batch MOQ

Rooted in a generational shoemaking legacy dating back to 1870, Konark Footwear India is an authoritative leader in high-precision insole engineering and polymer synthesis based in the premier footwear hub of Mundka, New Delhi.

We operate dedicated continuous rotary PU injection turntables, multi-daylight hydraulic presses, and high-speed CNC tooling centers to formulate microcellular cast polyurethane, slow-rebound viscoelastic memory foam, and ultra-resilient EVA/POE blends tailored to international footwear tech packs.

From ergonomic deep heel cups and anatomical arch stabilizers to customized Shore hardness tuning (30–60 Shore A), our turnkey OEM/ODM operations ensure rigorous batch consistency, SATRA testing compliance, and dependable worldwide logistics across 50+ export destinations.
