Preamble

Commitment to Global Data Protection

Konark Footwear India ("we", "our", or "the Company") recognizes the paramount importance of confidential commercial relationships and personal privacy. Operating as an industrial manufacturer and international exporter of polyurethane (PU), EVA, and ergonomic insoles, we routinely interact with European Union footwear brands, retail supply managers, customs agents, and overseas distributors.

This document explains our adherence to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR). Whether you connect with us via our digital portal, commercial email, or our dedicated B2B WhatsApp channel, your personal and corporate identifiers are safeguarded under strict international processing covenants.

Your Rights Under GDPR

Under Chapter III of the GDPR, European citizens and client representatives possess enforceable statutory rights concerning their personal information. Click each right below for details:

You are entitled to receive formal confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed, and to obtain a readable digital copy of all archived communication, specification records, and order histories.
If any of your business titles, dispatch destinations, VAT identifiers, or communication contacts are erroneous or incomplete, we will update records within five business days of written notice.
You may mandate the immediate deletion of your contact records where data is no longer necessary for the commercial purpose, or where consent is withdrawn, subject only to non-derogable export accounting regulations.
You can restrict data handling while a dispute over data accuracy or legitimate grounds is reviewed, ensuring no proactive outbound communication occurs during the assessment.
Receive your structured transaction history, purchase orders, and technical tech-pack references in a standard, machine-readable format (JSON/CSV) for transfer to any third party.
You may object at any time to receiving direct B2B marketing circulars, production updates, or seasonal footwear component catalogs from our sales desk.
